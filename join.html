Why become a member?

  • Twice-weekly peer support meetings
  • A voice in transplant policy through TRIO, a voting member of the OPTN
  • Education, resources, and mentor connections
  • Team Oklahoma and the Transplant Games community
  • Support for care partners, living donors, and donor families

Who can join?

Transplant recipients, people waiting or listed for transplant, care partners, living donors, and donor families. Everyone who cares about the transplant community is welcome.
